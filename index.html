Key principles
📈
Progressive overload
Every week adds reps, sets, or harder variations. This is what forces your muscles to grow.
🐢
Slow negatives
Lower yourself in 3 seconds on every push-up, squat, and dip. Slow negatives build more muscle.
🍗
Eat enough protein
Aim for 0.8–1g of protein per kg of bodyweight daily. Eggs, chicken, fish, beans, tofu all work.
😴
Sleep 7–8 hours
Muscles grow during sleep, not during the workout. Sleep is non-negotiable for muscle building.
💧
Stay hydrated
Drink water before, during, and after. Dehydration drops strength by 10–20%.
📓
Track your reps
Write down what you did each session. If you can't beat last week's numbers, something is off.
